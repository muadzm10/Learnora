STUDY TIPS

The Importance of Taking Study Notes

Organize information so it is easier to review later.

Introduction

Study notes can help learners organize information and identify important concepts. Effective notes do not need to contain every sentence from a lesson.

Use Your Own Words

Summarize ideas in language that you understand. This encourages active thinking.

Organize by Topic

Use headings and short sections to make information easier to find.

Add Examples

Examples can make abstract concepts easier to understand.

Review Your Notes

Return to your notes periodically and update them when you learn something new.

Conclusion

Clear, organized notes can make revision more focused and useful.
