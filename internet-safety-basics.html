Internet Safety Basics

Learn practical habits for protecting your information online.

Introduction

Using the internet safely involves protecting personal information and evaluating online content carefully.

Protect Account Credentials

Use strong, unique passwords and never share verification codes.

Check Website Addresses

Review a website's address before entering information or downloading files.

Be Careful with Unexpected Messages

Unexpected offers and urgent messages can be misleading. Verify their source independently.

Keep Software Updated

Updates can include important security improvements.

Conclusion

Careful browsing habits and account protection can reduce common online risks.
